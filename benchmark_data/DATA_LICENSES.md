# Data terms and attribution

The repository MIT license covers project software. Source datasets retain their provider's terms.

**DisGeNET.** Gene profiles derive from a local 2020 snapshot. Its primary publication specifies CC BY-NC-SA 4.0; retain attribution, noncommercial-use and share-alike requirements. Current platform access has separate rules. Cite Piñero et al., *Nucleic Acids Research* 48(D1), D845–D855, 2020. [Publication and terms](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7145631/) · [License](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc-sa/4.0/) · [Current access](https://support.disgenet.com/support/solutions/articles/202000089413-can-i-download-the-disgenet-database-)

**HMDD.** Mature-miRNA profiles use conservatively resolved HMDD records. Its tutorial permits free academic use and directs commercial users to contact the authors. Cite Cui et al., HMDD v4.0, *Nucleic Acids Research* 52(D1), D1327–D1332, 2024. That citation does not authenticate the version of every local export. [Official usage terms](https://www.cuilab.cn/hmdd)

**Interaction sources.** Identities and evidence labels combine [miRTarBase](https://awi.cuhk.edu.cn/miRTarBase/downloads/), [TarBase](https://dianalab.e-ce.uth.gr/tarbasev9), [TargetScan](https://www.targetscan.org/), [miRDB](https://mirdb.org/), and [miRWalk](http://mirwalk.umm.uni-heidelberg.de/). Their terms and citations remain applicable. Snapshot-specific license texts were incomplete, including permission to redistribute the contributed TarBase v9 snapshot. The composite master remains external; schemas and acquisition requirements accompany the fixed benchmark.

**miRBase.** The mature catalog uses public-domain/CC0 material. Cite Kozomara et al., *Nucleic Acids Research* 47(D1), D155–D162, 2019. [Official license](https://www.mirbase.org/download/CURRENT/LICENSE/) · [Publication](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6323917/)

**MeSH.** The existing Category C disease-name/tree-address table comes from the U.S. National Library of Medicine. Its release year is unknown; the baseline protocol identifies the exact file by checksum. [MeSH information](https://www.nlm.nih.gov/mesh/)
